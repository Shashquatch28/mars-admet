## Limits

- 8 endpoints is a small sample, and the endpoints are not independent draws. Treat the Friedman and sign-test p-values as a description of how consistent the ordering is, not as evidence about a population of endpoints.
- The Welch column sees only training-seed variation on one fixed test set. It does not see test-set sampling noise, which is large on the small sets (HIA 117 molecules, P-gp 245, BBB 394). With five seeds a tiny seed std makes almost any difference look significant; read the bootstrap intervals in `evaluation_results.md` beside it.
- Eight p-values are shown without a multiple-comparison correction; at alpha = 0.05 about one in twenty would pass by chance alone.
- On the absorption and distribution endpoints KERMT is multi-task and XGBoost is single-task, so a difference mixes the model with what the model saw in training.
- Not covered: the CYPs (the single-task KERMT baselines are not run), DILI (KERMT trained on the base pool, XGBoost on the augmented one) and toxicity.
